# Touch

From M1-05 on, the firmware reads the GT911 touch controller and feeds each touch to LVGL, so a finger on the panel presses buttons and swipes. A user sees the test screen's `Taps: N` button at the bottom centre count each tap. A developer or the harness can also inject taps and swipes over the debug console, read the click counter, print every touch report and gesture, and read the controller's report rate.
From M1-06 on, the test screen and its `Taps: N` button are gone, and the tiles of [UI pages](./ui-pages.md) take the taps and swipes.

## Sub-features

- `touch-address` logs `touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D` once per boot, before the touch driver starts. The line names the I2C address that answered. `0x14` would mean the address select line misbehaved.
- `touch-tap` answers `touch tap <x> <y>`, with x in 0 to 1023 and y in 0 to 599, by queueing a press and a release at that point. The reply comes after LVGL has handled both samples and the click they cause.
- `touch-swipe` answers `touch swipe <x1> <y1> <x2> <y2> <ms>`, with `ms` from 1 to 2000, by queueing a swipe from the first point to the second in 10 ms steps. The reply comes after LVGL has handled the samples and the gesture they cause, if they cause one.
- `touch-log` answers `touch log on` and `touch log off`. With the log on, each fresh real GT911 report prints `touch: x=<x> y=<y> pressed=<0|1>`, and each LVGL swipe gesture, real or injected, prints `touch: gesture left|right|up|down`. The log is off after boot.
- `touch-stats` answers `touch stats` with the controller counters, the injection counters, the last sample LVGL took, and the report rate of the latest finger contact.
- `touch-errors` answers a wrong argument count, a word that is not plain digits (`-1`, `5x`, `+5`, an empty word), or an unknown subcommand with a usage text, and a number outside its range with `out of range`. A queue too full for the gesture gives `queue full`, a real finger on the glass gives `finger down`, and a gesture LVGL did not take in time gives `not consumed in time`. Each reply has `ok` false. The usage and range checks run before the readiness check, so a malformed line gets its usage text even while the hardware is down.
- `touch-priority` hands LVGL an injected sample before any GT911 sample while one is queued. A gesture is queued whole or not at all, so it never loses its release. An injection is refused with `finger down` while a real finger is on the glass, because LVGL would see that press jump to the injected point and click the object under the finger.
- `ui-button` draws a rounded `Taps: N` button at the bottom centre of the test screen. It covers x 292 to 731 and y 440 to 539, and it logs `ui: click taps=N` for every click, with the touch log on or off. M1-06 replaces this screen.
  - **From M1-06 on.** There is no button and no test screen. A click on a tile logs `ui: click taps=N tile=<id>`, with the touch log on or off, and a regex that matches `ui: click taps=200` still matches it. [UI pages](./ui-pages.md) has the `ui-click` rules.
- `ui-state` answers `ui state` with `taps`, the clicks on the button since boot, and the button's box.
  - **From M1-06 on.** `ui state` moves to the `ui` component and replies with `page`, `pages`, `title`, `pressed_tile`, `taps`, `animating`, `transitions`, `transition_ms`, `transition_frames`, `last_ms`, `last_frames`, `dot_x`, `dot_pitch`, `dot_y`, `dot_active`, `dot_idle`, and `glow`. `taps` counts clicks on tiles. `button_x`, `button_y`, `button_w`, and `button_h` are gone.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Touch the panel. Tap the `Taps: N` button, or swipe across the screen.
  - **From M1-06 on.** Tap a tile, or swipe left and right to change the page.
- Open a serial terminal on the board's port and type `touch tap 512 490`, `touch swipe 900 200 100 200 300`, `touch log on`, `touch stats`, or `ui state` at `td> `.
  - **From M1-06 on.** Type `touch tap 143 128`, the centre of tile `f1`, in place of `touch tap 512 490`. Read the aim point of any tile with `ui tile <id>`.
- Run `$TD serial cmd "<line>"`, which sends the same line and prints the reply as JSON. Run `$TD serial capture --no-reset --send "<line>"` when the log lines that the touch causes are the evidence.

## Driving it with touchdeck.ps1

Preconditions:

- The build under test is M1-05 or later, and `$TD serial doctor` exits `0` with `build_matches` true.
- Evidence directory `E=<absolute path>/.verify/<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>-touch` is new, and Doctor's JSON is saved there as `doctor.json`.
- The hardware is up, so `$TD serial cmd "touch stats"` and `$TD serial cmd "ui state"` both exit `0`. Exit `1` with `touch not ready` or `display not ready` right after a reset means wait one second and run it again. If it persists past the boot window, read the boot log. A GT911 that never answered prints `touch: GT911 did not answer at 0x5D or 0x14` and `touchdeck: Touch failed to start`, and then every touch command answers `touch not ready` until the next reset.
- Nobody touches the panel during an injection recipe, because a real tap on the button adds a click.
- `taps` counts clicks since boot. Read `ui state` before each injection and judge the change in `taps`. After a reset with no click since, `taps` starts at 0, and the absolute values below hold.
  - **From M1-06 on.** `taps` counts clicks on tiles, a real tap on a tile adds a click, and a real swipe changes the page.

- **GT911 address at boot.** Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/boot --seconds 10 --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D"`. Exit `0`, with the address line after the banner in `$E/boot/serial.log`. The line prints once per boot, so let `capture` do the reset and don't use `--no-reset`. The reset also sets `taps` to 0. A line that says `0x14` fails the recipe, and the report quotes it.
- **Repeated boots.** Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/reboots --seconds 50 --no-reset --send "reboot" --send-after 1 --send-times 5 --send-every 9 --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D" --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D" --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D" --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D" --expect "touchdeck: TouchDeck firmware starting" --expect "touch: GT911 answered at 0x5D"`. Exit `0` only when five banners each have their own `0x5D` line after them. In PowerShell, `(Select-String -Path $E/reboots/serial.log -Pattern 'touch: GT911 answered at 0x').Count` and the same count with `0x5D` both print 5. Every boot then printed one address line, and none answered elsewhere.
- **Tap on the button.** Run `$TD serial cmd "ui state" > $E/before.json`, then `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 512 490" > $E/tap.json`, then `$TD serial cmd "ui state" > $E/after.json`. All three exit `0`. `tap.json` has `"op":"tap"`, `x` 512, `y` 490, `samples` 2, and a `deliver_us` between 1000 and 12000. `taps` in `after.json` is one more than in `before.json`, which makes it 1 after a reset. `button_x`, `button_y`, `button_w`, and `button_h` in `before.json` are 292, 440, 440, and 100, so (512, 490) is the centre of the button. `touch tap` replies late on purpose. The reply comes after LVGL has taken both samples and run one more read, so the click has already fired and the `ui state` that follows needs no wait.
  - **From M1-06 on.** There is no button, so aim at a tile. Run `$TD serial cmd "ui tile f1" > $E/tile.json`. It gives `page` 0, `x` 28, `y` 76, `w` 230, `h` 104, `cx` 143, and `cy` 128. Run `ui state`, then `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 143 128" > $E/tap.json`, then `ui state` again, with the deck on page 0. All exit `0`. `tap.json` has `"op":"tap"`, `x` 143, `y` 128, and `samples` 2. `taps` in the second `ui state` is one more than in the first. `ui state` has no `button_x`, `button_y`, `button_w`, or `button_h`. The click shows in a capture as `ui: click taps=N tile=f1`: `$TD serial capture --out $E/tap --seconds 5 --no-reset --send "touch tap 143 128" --expect "ui: click taps=\d+ tile=f1"`. Use `cx` and `cy` from `ui tile` if the layout changes.
- **Button label.** Right after the tap, run `$TD serial screenshot --scale 2 --out $E/after-tap.png > $E/after-tap.json`. Exit `0`, and the button label in the PNG reads `Taps: 1` after a reset.
  - **From M1-06 on.** No tile shows the count, so there is no label to read. The evidence of a click is `taps` in `ui state` and the `ui: click` line.
- **Tap outside the button.** Run `$TD serial cmd "ui state" > $E/miss-before.json`, then `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 64 300" > $E/miss-tap.json`, then `$TD serial cmd "ui state" > $E/miss.json`. All three exit `0`, because the injection worked. `taps` in `miss.json` equals `taps` in `miss-before.json`, and it stays 0 after a reset. The point (64, 300) is the centre of the first bar. Then run `touch tap` at `280 490`, `744 490`, `512 428`, and `512 552`, each at least 12 px outside the button's box, and read `ui state` once more. `taps` has not moved.
  - **From M1-06 on.** The bars and the button are gone, and a tap counts only on a tile. With page 0 showing, run `touch tap` at `266 128` (between `f1` and `f2`), `143 187` (between `f1` and `f5`), `10 300` (left of the grid), and `512 556` (below the grid), then read `ui state`. Each exits `0`, and `taps` has not moved. The M1-05 points `280 490` and `744 490` now land on tiles `ctrl` and `alt`, and they count.
- **Repeated taps.** Run `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 512 490" --repeat 100 > $E/hundred-taps.jsonl`. Exit `0`, with 100 lines that each have `ok` true. Then run `$TD serial cmd "ui state" > $E/hundred.json`. `taps` is 100 more than the value read before the run, and exactly 100 after a reset. Run `$TD serial cmd "touch stats" > $E/hundred-stats.json`. After a reset with no other injection, `injected` is 200 for two samples per tap, and `rejected` and `queued` are 0.
  - **From M1-06 on.** Send `touch tap 143 128` in place of `touch tap 512 490`. `taps` is 100 more, and `injected` is 200 more, which is exactly 200 after a reset with no other injection. At M1-06 the point (512, 490) lies in the gap between two tiles, so the old command counts no click.
- **Left swipe.** Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/swipe --seconds 6 --no-reset --send "touch log on" --send "touch swipe 900 200 100 200 300" --expect '@td \{"cmd":"touch","ok":true,"op":"log","on":true\}' --expect "touch: gesture left"`. Exit `0`. Left means the contact moves toward lower x. In PowerShell, `(Select-String -Path $E/swipe/serial.log -Pattern 'touch: gesture left').Count` prints 1, and the same count with `touch: gesture (right|up|down)` prints 0. `Select-String -Path $E/swipe/serial.log -Pattern '"op":"swipe"'` shows the reply, with `ok` true, a `samples` count near 32, and a `deliver_us` near 310000, which is `ms` plus about 10 ms. A swipe that logs `right` fails the recipe. Report it, and do not swap the coordinates to make it pass. Afterward, run `$TD serial cmd "touch log off"`. Its reply has `"on":false`.
  - **From M1-06 on.** The swipe also changes the page, because it starts on tile `f8` at (900, 200) and travels more than 96 px. Run `$TD serial cmd "ui goto 0"` before it, so it starts on page 0, and again after it. `touch: gesture left` still prints once, and `right`, `up`, and `down` never. A swipe that starts on a tile is still a swipe, and it counts no click.
- **Touch stats.** Run `$TD serial cmd "touch stats" --repeat 2 --interval 2 > $E/stats.jsonl`. Exit `0`. `address` is `0x5D`, and `pressed` is false while nobody touches the panel. `polls` rises by about 200 between the two lines, because LVGL reads once every 10 ms. Report `errors`, which counts failed I2C reads, when it is above 0. `injected`, `rejected`, and `queued` count only injected samples.
- **Tap-to-click time.** Run `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 512 490" --repeat 50 --time > $E/taps50.jsonl 2> $E/rtt50.txt`. Exit `0`, and `taps` rises by 50. In PowerShell, run `$d = Get-Content $E/taps50.jsonl | ForEach-Object { ($_ | ConvertFrom-Json).deliver_us } | Sort-Object; $d[[int][math]::Ceiling(0.95 * $d.Count) - 1]`. It prints the p95 of `deliver_us` in microseconds, and the plan's bound is 50000. `$d[0]` and `$d[-1]` are the minimum and the maximum. `deliver_us` runs from the moment the firmware queues the tap to the moment LVGL takes the release sample, and the click fires right after that sample. It holds the wait for LVGL's next 10 ms read, so it lies between 0 and about 10000 while LVGL is not busy drawing, and the 50000 bound has wide margin. A tap's press and release share one read, so the figure has no render of the pressed button in it. It leaves out the time to send and parse the console line before the queueing, the click handler and the label redraw after the release, and the reply's trip back to the host. `rtt_ms` in `rtt50.txt` is the host's whole round trip, so it is always larger. Report both figures and say what `deliver_us` leaves out.
  - **From M1-06 on.** Aim at tile `f1`: `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 143 128" --repeat 50 --time > $E/taps50.jsonl 2> $E/rtt50.txt`. `taps` rises by 50, and the p95 of `deliver_us` is read the same way. The M1-06 plan rule is that the p95 grows by no more than 10 ms over the parent's, measured with the recipe above. [UI pages](./ui-pages.md) has the batches.
- **200 taps during the heartbeat.** Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/heartbeat --seconds 70 --send-after 6 --send "touch tap 512 490" --send-times 200 --send-every 0.25 --expect "ui: click taps=200" --expect "touchdeck: alive 30, free heap \d+ bytes"`. The reset gives a clean `taps` and a touch log that is off. Exit `0` means the 200th click logged and the heartbeat still ticked after the load. In PowerShell, `(Select-String -Path $E/heartbeat/serial.log -Pattern '>> sent').Count` prints 200, and the same count with `>> send failed` prints 0. Then run `$t = Select-String -Path $E/heartbeat/serial.log -Pattern 'I \((\d+)\) touchdeck: alive \d+' | ForEach-Object { [int]$_.Matches[0].Groups[1].Value }; 1..($t.Count - 1) | ForEach-Object { $t[$_] - $t[$_ - 1] } | Measure-Object -Minimum -Maximum`. `Minimum` is at least 1800 and `Maximum` is at most 2200, by the device clock. Last, run `$TD serial cmd "ui state"`. `taps` is 200.
  - **From M1-06 on.** Send `touch tap 143 128` in place of `touch tap 512 490`, because (512, 490) lies in the gap between two tiles and counts no click. The 200th click logs `ui: click taps=200 tile=f1`, which the regex `ui: click taps=200` still matches, and `ui state` reads `taps` 200 after the run.
- **Frame rate with touch.** Run `$TD serial cmd "perf fps 5" --timeout 8 > $E/fps-head-1.json` as in the display recipe, with no injection or screenshot running. Do the same on the parent build, which has no touch. Alternate parent and head three times each, and flash each side per the boot recipe in `SKILL.md`. Compare the mean `fps` of the three head runs with the mean of the three parent runs. The plan fails the head when it is more than 10 percent lower.
- **Board still flashes.** Run `$TD serial doctor > $E/doctor-end.json`, then `$TD esptool chip-id > $E/chip-id.txt`. Doctor exits `0`, and `chip-id.txt` holds the board's MAC.
- **Operator only, GT911 report rate.** An agent cannot run this alone, because `contact_hz` counts only real controller reports and never injected samples. Report it as pending operator unless the operator held the finger. The operator presses one finger still on a bar, away from the button, for 5 seconds. While the finger is down, run `$TD serial cmd "touch tap 512 490"` once. It exits `1` with `finger down`, `taps` does not move, and `queued` stays 0. Then the operator lifts the finger and touches nothing else. Run `$TD serial cmd "touch stats" > $E/stats-hold.json` at once. `contact_hz` stays after the finger lifts, until the next contact. `pressed_frames` is above 0 and `contact_ms` is near 5000. Report `contact_hz`, and the plan fails it under 60. It should sit near `contact_frames*1000/contact_ms`, and a large gap is worth reporting.
  - **From M1-06 on.** The operator presses the finger still on a tile, such as `f12`, in place of a bar. While it is down, `touch tap 143 128` still exits `1` with `finger down` and queues nothing. A finger that lifts inside a tile is a click, so `taps` rises by 1 when the operator lifts, where it did not move at M1-05. Read `ui state` before and after, and subtract that click.
- **Operator only, four corners.** An agent cannot run this alone, because no injected sample prints a `touch: x=` line. Report it as pending operator unless the operator did the touching. Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/corners --seconds 40 --no-reset --send "touch log on" --expect "touch: x=\d+ y=\d+ pressed=1"`. Within the 40 seconds, the operator taps the top left, top right, bottom right, and bottom left corners in that order. Exit `0` shows only that a finger reported. In PowerShell, run `Select-String -Path $E/corners/serial.log -Pattern 'touch: x=(\d+) y=(\d+) pressed=1' | ForEach-Object { '{0} {1}' -f $_.Matches[0].Groups[1].Value, $_.Matches[0].Groups[2].Value }`. Each tap prints a run of lines. The four runs sit within 20 px of (0, 0), (1023, 0), (1023, 599), and (0, 599) in that order. A run far from its corner means the orientation or the mirroring is wrong. Afterward, run `$TD serial cmd "touch log off"`.
- **Operator only, real swipes.** An agent cannot run this alone, because the swipe needs a finger. Report it as pending operator unless the operator did the swiping. Run `$TD serial capture --out $E/real-swipe --seconds 30 --no-reset --send "touch log on" --expect "touch: gesture left" --expect "touch: gesture right"`. Within the 30 seconds, the operator swipes left once and then right once, away from the button. Exit `0` shows a left gesture and later a right gesture. Count the `touch: gesture` lines in `$E/real-swipe/serial.log` and report any extra `up` or `down`. Afterward, run `$TD serial cmd "touch log off"`.
  - **From M1-06 on.** The operator can swipe anywhere, including from a tile. The left swipe changes the page to 1 and the right swipe changes it back to 0, so `ui state` reads `page` 0 afterward.
- **Proof.** Keep `$E/doctor.json`, `$E/boot/`, the before and after JSON of each injection, the injection replies, each capture's `serial.log` and `summary.json`, and the PNG. Name in the report what only the operator can confirm. That is corner accuracy, orientation, mirroring, real swipes, the GT911 report rate, and the feel of a finger.

## Gotchas

- Injection proves that LVGL and the UI react. A tap or swipe enters at LVGL's input device, after the GT911 and the glass, so a pass says nothing about the controller, its wiring, or the panel.
- Accuracy, orientation, mirroring, and feel of a real finger need the operator. Report them as pending operator, never as verified from an injected touch, a log, or a screenshot. An injected sample never prints a `touch: x=` line and never counts in `contact_hz`, so those two show a real finger.
- `touch ...` and `ui state` answer `ok` false until the hardware is up. The replies are `touch not ready` and `display not ready`, and `cmd` exits `1`. That is a timing fault of the run, not a failed injection, so wait and run it again.
- The board lock. Only the holder of `.verify/hardware.lock` opens the port. `taps` is one counter for the whole board, so another run's tap changes your count, and `capture` holds the port exclusively anyway. Take the lock before the first read and release it after the last.
- `help` lists `touch` and `ui`, because they are registered in the console table with the other commands. Take the argument forms from this file. `help` only confirms the names.
- `touch tap` and `touch swipe` block the console until LVGL has taken their samples and run one more read. A tap blocks it for up to about 20 ms and a swipe for its `ms` plus about 10 to 20 ms. Run no other `cmd` meanwhile. `--send` lines in a `capture` queue and run in order.
- A swipe over 2000 ms is refused with `ok` false. The default `--timeout` of 3 seconds in `cmd` covers every swipe the firmware accepts.
- A `not consumed in time` reply leaves the samples queued. They play out later and move `taps`. Read `touch stats` until `queued` is 0 before the next count. A `queue full` reply queues nothing, and `rejected` counts it.
- `touch log on` stays on until `touch log off` or a reset. With it on, every fresh real report prints a line, and a report with no finger prints `pressed=0`. Turn it off after a recipe that needs it, and run the heartbeat check with it off.
- While injected samples are queued, LVGL gets those and no GT911 sample. A real finger on the button between injections still counts as a click.
- `capture` checks its `--send` schedule once per read, and a read lasts up to 0.2 seconds. Sends land on a 0.2 second grid, so use a `--send-every` of at least 0.25. 200 sends at 0.25 take about 50 seconds. Set `--seconds` to `--send-after` plus that time plus 10.
- Start a swipe outside the button's box, x 292 to 731 and y 440 to 539, because a press on the button starts a click.
  - **From M1-06 on.** A swipe may start on a tile or at y 300, and it is still a swipe, because a press that moves more than 16 px is not a tap. A left or right swipe that travels 96 px now changes the page, so run `ui goto 0` before and after a recipe that swipes. A press that lands while a slide is still running stops the slide and is never a tap.
- LVGL raises a gesture only when each 10 ms step moves at least 3 px (`gesture_min_velocity`) and the whole swipe moves more than 50 px (`gesture_min_distance`). A `touch swipe` reply with `ok` true proves that the samples reached LVGL, not that a gesture fired. The `touch: gesture` log line proves the gesture. The recipe's 800 px in 300 ms is 26 px per step. A swipe of 400 px in 2000 ms is 2 px per step and raises nothing.
- A poll sees at most one fresh report, so `contact_hz` cannot read above the 100 polls per second. Treat it as the report rate seen at 10 ms polling, a floor for the controller's own rate.
- Do not inject while anyone touches the panel. The firmware refuses with `finger down`, and a real finger that lands during an injection can click the object under it.
- The `Taps: N` button, `taps`, and the aim point (512, 490) belong to the M1-05 test screen. M1-06 replaces the screen, so read the button's box from `ui state` and take the targets for later screens from their own entries.
  - **From M1-06 on.** M1-06 replaces the screen with the deck. The `Taps: N` button and the aim point (512, 490) are gone, and `taps` counts clicks on tiles. Take a tile's aim point from `ui tile <id>`, which holds only while that tile's page shows, and take the `ui` argument forms from [UI pages](./ui-pages.md).
- From M1-12 on, a board left idle longer than the idle timeout has its screen off, and the first injected tap or swipe only wakes it, with no click and no gesture. Run `$TD serial cmd "power state"` first and wake the screen if it says `OFF`. [Idle](./idle.md) has the rules.
