# UI pages

From M1-06 on, the firmware shows the deck: a title at the top left, a grid of rounded dark tiles, and a row of page dots at the bottom. There are two pages, `F-Keys` (page 0, four rows of four tiles) and `Media` (page 1, two rows of three tiles). A swipe left or right slides to the other page, a press lights a tile with a glow, and a swipe past either end bounces and changes nothing. The tiles send nothing at M1-06. From M1-10 on, the `F-Keys` tiles send keys, and [F-Keys page](./fkey-page.md) covers that. A developer or the harness can read the deck's state, jump to a page, look up a tile's box, hold a press on the glass, and read the transition timing, all over the debug console.

## Sub-features

- `ui-boot-page` starts the deck on page 0, `F-Keys`, with two dots and the first one lit. Nothing is saved across a reset, so every boot starts there.
- `ui-theme` draws from named RGB565 tokens, listed here as decimals: `bg` 2114, `tile` 6406, `tile_down` 10765, `text` 61311, `title` 31795, `dot_idle` 16971, `dot_active` 19743, and `glow` 19743. In hex they are `0842`, `1906`, `2A0D`, `EF7F`, `7C33`, `424B`, `4D1F`, and `4D1F`. Every fill is one flat token, with no gradient. `ui state` reports `dot_idle`, `dot_active`, and `glow`.
- `ui-tiles` lays rounded tiles (radius 20, 16 px apart) on a grid that covers x 28 to 995 and y 76 to 539. `F-Keys` holds `f1` to `f12`, then `shift`, `ctrl`, `alt`, and `win`, four to a row, each 230 by 104 px. `Media` holds `prev`, `playpause`, and `next` in the first row and `mute`, `voldown`, and `volup` in the second, each 312 by 224 px.
- `ui-tile-box` answers `ui tile <id>` with the tile's `page`, `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, `cx`, and `cy`, and from M1-10 on its label as `name`. The box is where the tile sits on screen while its page shows, and `cx`, `cy` is the aim point for a tap or a hold.
- `ui-dots` draws one dot per page. Dot `i` is centred at (`dot_x` + `i` * `dot_pitch`, `dot_y`) from `ui state`, which is (495, 580) and (529, 580) with two pages. The lit dot belongs to the showing page and its centre pixel equals `dot_active`. The other dot's centre pixel equals `dot_idle`. The lit dot is also wider than the idle one.
- `ui-swipe` changes the page on the move where the finger's horizontal travel from the press reaches 96 px, while the finger is still down, and slides the pages in 260 ms. The pages do not follow the finger. The title changes when the pages are halfway across. A swipe left goes to the next page and a swipe right to the previous one.
- `ui-bounce` answers a swipe left on the last page, or right on the first page, by moving the pages 48 px toward the swipe in 110 ms and back in 150 ms. The page does not change and does not wrap.
- `ui-drag-rules` treats a press that moves more than 16 px in either axis as no longer a tap. When its horizontal travel is at least its vertical travel it is a drag, which swipes at 96 px and does nothing before that. Otherwise it is a vertical move, which never changes the page and never becomes a tap, whatever it does later.
- `ui-press-glow` gives a pressed tile a 3 px border in the `glow` color, a glow shadow, and the `tile_down` fill, over 120 ms. `ui state` names the tile in `pressed_tile` until the finger lifts or the press stops being a tap.
- `ui-click` counts a click when a finger lifts on a tile after staying inside the 16 px radius. It raises `taps` and logs `ui: click taps=N tile=<id>`. A tap that lands on no tile counts nothing. A press that lands while a slide is still running stops the slide and is never a click, but it can still swipe.
- `ui-state` answers `ui state` with `page`, `pages`, `tile_count` (from M1-10 on, the tiles on the showing page), `title` (the showing page's title), `pressed_tile`, `taps`, `animating`, `transitions`, `transition_ms`, `transition_frames`, `last_ms`, `last_frames`, `dot_x`, `dot_pitch`, `dot_y`, `dot_active`, `dot_idle`, and `glow`. `pressed_tile` is an empty string when no tile is pressed, and `animating` is true while a slide or a bounce runs.
- `ui-goto` answers `ui goto <page>` with `{"cmd":"ui","ok":true,"op":"goto","page":N}` at once and slides to the page the way a swipe does. A `ui: page shown` line follows when the slide ends, about 260 ms later. `ui goto` to the page that shows starts no slide.
- `ui-hold` answers `ui hold <x> <y> <ms>` by queueing a press at that point for `ms` (1 to 2000, rounded down to whole 10 ms steps, at least one) and then a release at the same point. It replies after the press has registered and the 120 ms glow transition has finished, about 0.2 seconds in, with `pressed_tile` set to the tile LVGL pressed, or an empty string when the point is on no tile. The hold runs on after the reply, so `ui state` and `$TD serial screenshot` can run during it.
- `ui-transition-stats` adds up the page changes that ran their whole slide in `transitions`, `transition_ms`, and `transition_frames`, so `transition_frames * 1000 / transition_ms` is the frame rate during transitions. `last_ms` and `last_frames` hold the latest such slide. A bounce, and a slide that a new touch cut short, are in neither. A slide that ends logs `ui: page shown page=N ms=M frames=F`, and a bounce that ends logs `ui: bounce done page=N ms=M frames=F`. A slide that a new touch or a `ui goto` cut short logs `ui: slide cut page=N ms=M frames=F` instead, so a search for `ui: page shown` finds only whole slides.
- `ui-errors` answers an unknown subcommand, or `ui state` with extra words, with `usage: ui state | goto <page> | tile <id> | hold <x> <y> <ms>`. A known subcommand answers `display not ready` before the display starts, ahead of its own argument checks. `ui goto` answers `usage: ui goto <page>`, `out of range` for a page past the last one, and `display busy` when LVGL does not free up in 3 seconds. `ui tile` answers `usage: ui tile <id>` and `no such tile`. `ui hold` answers `usage: ui hold <x 0-1023> <y 0-599> <ms 1-2000>` for a bad word, a number out of range, or 0 ms, and `touch not ready`, `finger down`, `hold queued` while an earlier injection is still queued (two `ui hold` lines sent back to back in one `capture` answer with the hold's reply and then `hold queued`), `queue full`, or `hold refused`. Each reply has `ok` false.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Power the board or press RESET. The panel shows the deck on page 0, `F-Keys`, with two dots and the first one lit.
- Swipe left to reach `Media`, and swipe right to come back. A swipe past either end nudges the pages and they spring back.
- Press a tile. It lights with a glow while the finger is down, and a lift without moving counts a click.
- Open a serial terminal on the board's port and type `ui state`, `ui goto 1`, `ui tile f1`, or `ui hold 143 128 1500` at `td> `.
- Run `$TD serial cmd "<line>"`, which sends the same line and prints the reply as JSON. Run `$TD serial capture --no-reset --send "<line>"` when the log lines that the touch causes are the evidence.

## Driving it with touchdeck.ps1

Preconditions:

- The build under test is M1-06 or later, and `$TD serial doctor` exits `0` with `build_matches` true.
- Evidence directory `E=<absolute path>/.verify/<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>-ui-pages` is new, and Doctor's JSON is saved there as `doctor.json`.
- The hardware is up, so `$TD serial cmd "ui state"` and `$TD serial cmd "touch stats"` both exit `0`. Exit `1` with `display not ready` or `touch not ready` right after a reset means wait one second and run it again.
- Nobody touches the panel during an injection recipe, because a real touch adds a click or changes the page.
- Each recipe starts on page 0 at rest unless it says otherwise. Run `$TD serial cmd "ui goto 0"`, then `$TD serial cmd "ui state"`, and require `page` 0, `animating` false, and an empty `pressed_tile`. When `animating` is true, run `ui state` again.
- `taps` counts tile clicks since boot. Read `ui state` before each injection and judge the change in `taps`. After a reset with no click since, `taps` starts at 0.
- Pixel reader, run once per PowerShell session: `function px($f, $w, $x, $y) { $b = [IO.File]::ReadAllBytes($f); $o = ($y * $w + $x) * 2; $b[$o] + 256 * $b[$o + 1] }`. It prints pixel (`$x`, `$y`) of a `--raw` file as a decimal RGB565 value, with `$w` the image width, 1024 at scale 1 and 256 at scale 4. Compare it with the `ui state` fields `dot_active`, `dot_idle`, and `glow`, and with the token list under `ui-theme`.

The plan fails the head when the frames per second drop under 15. Report `$n`, the mean slide time, and the rate. Frames come in whole numbers over a slide of about 260 ms, so one frame more or less moves a single slide's rate by about 4 fps. That is why the rate comes from the totals of 20 slides and not from one `last_frames`. The swipe-to-page time comes from the device clock. ## Gotchas

- A screenshot is one frame of LVGL's render of the active screen. It cannot show animation smoothness, flicker, tearing, or backlight state, and it cannot show the feel of a finger. A pass on the press frames or on a slide's frames says where things were, not that the motion was smooth. Report all of those as not verified unless the operator looked at the real panel.
- A tile's box from `ui tile` holds only while that tile's page shows. A tap aimed at `prev` while page 0 shows lands on whatever page 0 has at that point, which can be a gap or another tile. Take the page into account before every aim.
- Only a tile counts a click. A tap in a gap, in a margin, or on the title counts nothing and logs nothing, and a swipe's release is never a click.
- A press that lands while a slide or a bounce still runs stops it, is never a tap, and can still swipe. The slide takes 260 ms, and `animating` in `ui state` says when it ended. A press back-to-back with `ui goto` or with another swipe, as in a `capture` with two `--send` lines, can land inside a slide.
- A slide that something cut short logs `ui: slide cut page=N ms=M frames=F` and is in no total. A bounce logs `ui: bounce done`, is in no total, and sets `animating` for its 260 ms.
- Every left or right swipe that travels 96 px changes the page, so the M1-05 swipe recipes now leave the deck on page 1. Run `ui goto 0` before them, and again after. Start swipes at y 300, which is a gap on both pages, or on a tile, and both work. A press that leaves the 16 px radius mostly vertically never swipes, even if it later travels 96 px sideways.
- The pages do not follow the finger. The page changes on the move that reaches 96 px, while the finger is still down, so an injected swipe changes the page before its release and a swipe shorter than that changes nothing.
- A page-changing `touch swipe` takes its `ms` plus about 300 milliseconds to reply, because the slide redraws the screen and LVGL then reads one sample per frame. The owner's 10 swipes of 300 ms replied in 571 to 604 ms, median about 590 ms (`deliver_us`). The reply comes after the slide has finished, so `ui state` right after it reads `animating` false and the new page. The default 3 second `cmd` timeout covers a 300 ms swipe, and the firmware gives up on its own after `ms` plus 1 second with `not consumed in time`.
- `ui hold` replies after about 0.2 seconds, and the press runs on. Every `$TD` call starts pwsh and activates ESP-IDF before it opens the port, so a separate call cannot be counted on to land inside a hold. Use `--background "ui hold ..."` on `$TD serial screenshot`, or two `--send` lines in a `capture`.
- A hold can last a little longer than its `ms`, because LVGL takes one sample per frame while a frame is drawing and the press redraws the tile for 120 ms. It ends with a release at the same point, which clicks the tile and raises `taps` by 1.
- A 2000 ms hold queues 202 of the 256 queue slots, so a second long `ui hold` right after the first answers `queue full`, and `rejected` in `touch stats` counts it. A `touch tap` or `touch swipe` sent during a hold waits behind it. It answers `not consumed in time` when the hold outlasts its own timeout, which is 1 second for a tap and `ms` plus 1 second for a swipe. Its samples then play out after the hold and can click.
- `ui hold` answers a bad word, a number out of range, or 0 ms with the usage text, where `touch tap` answers `out of range`. It answers `finger down` while a real finger is on the glass and queues nothing.
- The glow pixels read `glow` once the 120 ms transition has finished, and `ui hold` replies only after that. A fill probe at (tile x + 12, `cy`) reads `tile` at rest and `tile_down` while pressed. Probe `f1`, whose label is far from the probe. A label glyph can sit on a probe pixel on another tile, and a glyph is anti-aliased, so no recipe probes `text` or `title`.
- The dots are `dot_idle` and `dot_active` only after their 160 ms transition. It starts when the page changes and ends before the 260 ms slide does, so read the dots when `animating` is false.
- Log lines (`ui: page shown`, `ui: bounce done`, `ui: click`) go through stdio and can be dropped while the display redraws, which is when these lines print. Read numbers from `ui state` and treat the lines as secondary. `capture` stamps a line when its read returns, which can be up to 0.2 seconds late, and neighbouring lines can share a stamp. Take gaps shorter than a second from the device clock, `I (<ms>)`.
- `transition_frames * 1000 / transition_ms` covers whole slides only. A single slide's `last_frames` is a small whole number, so judge the 15 fps rule on the totals of 20.
- Measured at M1-06 on the board: 10 slides ran 5 frames each in 289 ms on average, which is 17.3 frames per second over the totals, and 20 alternating slides gave 17.4. `perf fps 5` on the F-Keys page read 15, because it redraws the whole screen and a slide redraws only the sliding band. A screen with every tile hidden redrew at 20 frames per second, so the panel's flush path sets a floor of about 50 ms a frame that no UI choice removes.
- `perf fps` redraws the whole deck on every tick. Its `fps` is the deck's and is not comparable with the eight-bar figure of M1-04 without saying so.
- The first swipes may allocate once. If `free_heap` drops more than 2048 bytes over the 100 swipes, run lane 8 again without a reboot and report both runs. A one-time allocation shows in the first run only, and a leak shows in both.
- The deck always boots to page 0. `ui goto` and swipes are not saved. A reset also zeroes `taps` and the transition totals.
- `taps` is one counter for the whole board, and a `ui goto` slide counts in the transition totals like a swipe's. Do not run `ui goto` inside the window of the transition recipe.
